# MODEL CARD: HyperTwin
- **Intended use:** research/demo of a patient-specific twin. NOT for diagnosis or treatment.
- **Data:** 150 synthetic patients x 90 days (seed 2026): EHR fields (age, sex, BMI, antihypertensive count, family history, last clinic SBP) + daily morning BP, sleep, steps. ~10% missing, ~2% implausible readings injected.
- **Model:** (1) within-patient regression of SBP on sleep/steps; (2) OLS EHR prior for each patient's level; (3) local-level Kalman filter, Q chosen by 1-step MAE on training patients; forecast is Gaussian, risk = P(SBP >= target).
- **Evaluation:** parameters fit on 90 training patients; tested forward in time on 60 disjoint patients (14-day warm-up, 4,024 predictions). Baselines: last valid reading, 7-day mean (SD for baseline probabilities taken from training patients). Headline twin uses only past data (past-average sleep/steps). Results (MAE / AUROC / Brier): twin 4.25 / 0.937 / 0.075; 7-day mean 4.33 / 0.935 / 0.076; last reading 5.44 / 0.904 / 0.099. With same-day covariates assumed known: twin 3.96 / 0.947 / 0.070 vs adjusted 7-day mean 4.01 / 0.946 / 0.071. Single seed, no confidence intervals.
- **Assumptions:** sleep/steps effects are invented in the generator (-1.2 mmHg/h, -0.6 mmHg/1000 steps). Target 135 mmHg follows the guideline home-BP threshold, but guidelines apply it to multi-day averages; using it for a single morning reading is a proxy.
- **Limitations:** results measure agreement with the synthetic generator, not clinical validity. The twin's edge over a 7-day mean is small. Held-out patients come from the same generator as training patients. No real-world, device, or clinician validation. No treatment-effect claims.
